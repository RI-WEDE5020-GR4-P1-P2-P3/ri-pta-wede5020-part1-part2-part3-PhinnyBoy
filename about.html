About QuickFix SA

Our History

QuickFix SA is a fictional South African property services company created to make property-related services easier to access.

The idea was created because tenants, homeowners and property owners can sometimes struggle to find reliable professionals when property problems occur.

QuickFix SA also provides a property marketplace where users can discover properties available for sale.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make finding properties and solving property maintenance problems faster, easier and more convenient.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted South African online platform for discovering properties and accessing reliable property maintenance services.

Who We Serve

  • Property buyers
  • Tenants
  • Homeowners
  • Landlords
  • Property sellers
  • Repair professionals
  • Small businesses

Our Team

QuickFix SA is supported by a team responsible for customer support, property listings, service providers and website administration.
